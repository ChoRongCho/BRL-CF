\section{Human Evaluation Procedure}~\label{appx:user}

This appendix describes the participant briefing and tablet interaction used for the human evaluation in Sec.~\ref{exp:human_eval}. Participants receive domain information that includes task goals and available actions, then answer robot questions according to their own judgment. The physical platforms and task domains match the VLM evaluation.

\subsection{Domain Information}~\label{appx:user_scenarios}
In WasteSorting, participants receive information about the four waste categories and the corresponding bins. The available actions detect waste objects, pick an object, and place the held object in a selected bin. In TomatoHarvesting, participants receive information about ripe and unripe tomatoes and the distinction between fresh and rotten tomatoes. The available actions navigate between locations, detect tomatoes at a stem, pick a tomato, scan a held tomato, and place or discard the tomato. The task goal requires fresh ripe tomatoes to be harvested, rotten tomatoes to be discarded, and unripe tomatoes to remain on the stem.

\subsection{Tablet Interaction}~\label{appx:user_assignment}
The tablet interface in Fig.~\ref{fig:human_tablet_interface} displays robot camera views and the current query. Ours presents a question about a selected state fact with Boolean response options. KnowNo presents candidate actions and requests an action choice. Participants choose a response through the tablet, and the robot continues execution after the response is submitted. Ours can request several state confirmations before the confidence threshold is met. KnowNo requests one action choice per trigger.

\subsection{Trial Schedule}
The planned evaluation comprises five participants with two trials per domain and method for each participant. This schedule yields $10$ trials per domain and method and $40$ trials in total. The current quantitative human results use the available $24$-trial dataset with three participants and six trials per domain and method. Participant demographics and the final trial schedule remain to be verified after data collection.
